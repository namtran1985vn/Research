# Session 1 — What Is a Ratio? · ~30 min

**Week:** 01
**Khan Academy:** 6th grade → Unit 1 (Ratios, rates & percentages) → "Intro to ratios"
**Goal:** Understand that a ratio compares two quantities, and write a ratio three ways.

## 1. Warm-up — 3 min

Put **3 red pens and 2 blue pens** on the table. Ask: "How many red? How many blue?" Then: "Today we're learning how to compare those two numbers — it's called a **ratio**."

## 2. Teach the concept — 7 min

"A ratio compares two numbers. 3 red pens to 2 blue pens — the ratio of red to blue is **3 to 2**. Three ways to write it: `3 : 2`, `3/2`, or *'3 to 2'*."

Stress **order matters**: red : blue = 3:2 is different from blue : red = 2:3. Whatever is named first gets written first.

## 3. Khan Academy — 8 min

Watch together: *"Intro to ratios"* and *"Basic ratios."* Pause and explain anything unclear.

## 4. Practice — 8 min

Do "Basic ratios" exercise on Khan, or these:

1. 4 apples, 6 oranges. Ratio apples : oranges?
2. 10 girls, 15 boys. Ratio girls : boys, three ways.
3. 2 cups flour, 1 cup sugar. Ratio flour : sugar?
4. 5 dogs, 3 cats. Ratio cats : dogs? (watch order!)
5. 8 candies, 3 red. Ratio red : total?

**Answers:** 1) 4:6  ·  2) 10:15, 10/15, "10 to 15"  ·  3) 2:1  ·  4) 3:5  ·  5) 3:8

## 5. Check understanding + close — 4 min

Ask: "A class has 12 girls and 8 boys. Ratio girls : boys, three ways?"
Correct = 12:8, 12/8, "12 to 8" → mastered.
Preview tomorrow: "**Equivalent ratios** — how `2:3` and `6:9` are the same."

## Common mistake to watch for

Writing the order backward (asked for cats : dogs, writes dogs : cats). Gentle reminder: whatever is named first gets written first.

## Stretch (if she finishes fast)

Show "Equivalent ratios" video and start Session 2 early.

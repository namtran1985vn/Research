# Accelerated Math I — 32-Week Roadmap

Scope: all of US 6th-grade math + first half of 7th-grade math.
Output goal: ready for Algebra 1 in grade 8.

## PART A — Grade 6

### Unit 1 — Ratios, rates & percentages
- Week 1: Intro to ratios; equivalent ratios; ratio tables
- Week 2: Unit rates, speed, comparing unit rates
- Week 3: Percents; convert between fractions / decimals / percents
- Week 4: Unit conversion; ratio word problems; **Unit 1 review**

### Unit 2 — Arithmetic operations
- Week 5: Dividing fractions by fractions
- Week 6: +/−/×/÷ with decimals
- Week 7: GCF, LCM; **Unit 2 review**

### Unit 3 — Negative numbers & rationals
- Week 8: Negative numbers, number line, opposites
- Week 9: Absolute value; comparing & ordering rationals
- Week 10: Coordinate plane (4 quadrants); **Unit 3 review**

### Unit 4 — Variables & expressions
- Week 11: Exponents & order of operations
- Week 12: Writing & evaluating algebraic expressions
- Week 13: Distributive property; equivalent expressions; **Unit 4 review**

### Unit 5 — Equations & inequalities
- Week 14: One-step equations
- Week 15: Inequalities; representing on a number line
- Week 16: Dependent vs independent variables; **Unit 5 review**

### Unit 6 — Geometry
- Week 17: Area of triangles, quadrilaterals, polygons
- Week 18: Volume of rectangular prisms (fractional edges)
- Week 19: Surface area & nets; **Unit 6 review**

### Unit 7 — Statistics & data
- Week 20: Statistical questions; dot plots, histograms, box plots
- Week 21: Mean, median, mode; range, IQR, MAD
- Week 22: Review + **MIDPOINT TEST (end of grade 6 content)**

## PART B — First half of Grade 7

### Unit 8 — Proportional relationships
- Week 23: Unit rates with fractions; constant of proportionality (k)
- Week 24: Graphing proportional relationships; y = kx
- Week 25: Advanced percent problems (markup/discount, tax, interest); **Unit 8 review**

### Unit 9 — Operations with rational numbers
- Week 26: Adding & subtracting rationals (positive/negative)
- Week 27: Multiplying & dividing rationals
- Week 28: Multi-step problems with rationals; **Unit 9 review**

### Unit 10 — Expressions, equations & inequalities
- Week 29: Equivalent expressions (distribute, combine like terms, factor)
- Week 30: Two-step equations (px + q = r)
- Week 31: Two-step inequalities; **Unit 10 review**

### Week 32 — Comprehensive review + **FINAL TEST**

Next course: Accelerated Math II (second half of grade 7 + grade 8), then Algebra 1.

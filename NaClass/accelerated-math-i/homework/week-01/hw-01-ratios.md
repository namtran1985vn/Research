# Homework 1 — Ratios

*~15–20 min. Write each ratio on paper. The quantity named first comes first.*

1. A fruit bowl has 5 bananas and 4 apples. Ratio **bananas : apples**?
2. A parking lot has 7 cars and 3 trucks. Ratio **cars : trucks**, three ways.
3. A box has 6 blue and 9 green markers. Ratio **green : blue**?
4. A team has 8 forwards and 11 defenders. Ratio **defenders : forwards**?
5. A bag has 4 red, 5 yellow, 6 green marbles. Ratio **red : yellow**?
6. Same bag as #5: how many marbles total? Ratio **red : total**?
7. Recipe uses 3 cups flour for every 2 eggs. Ratio **eggs : flour**?
8. Class of 24, 14 are girls. How many boys? Ratio **boys : girls**?
9. Garden has 12 roses and 8 tulips. Ratio **tulips : all flowers**?
10. Look around. Count two kinds of things. Write the ratio two ways.

---

## Answer key (for teacher)

1. **5:4**
2. **7:3**, **7/3**, **"7 to 3"**
3. **9:6** (green first)
4. **11:8** (defenders first)
5. **4:5**
6. Total = 15. **4:15**
7. **2:3** (order flipped from wording — watch for this)
8. 10 boys. **10:14**
9. Total = 20. **8:20**
10. Varies. Check order + two forms.

**Note on common variations:** Several answers reduce (9:6→3:2, 8:20→2:5, 10:14→5:7). Unreduced is correct for today — simplifying is Session 2.

**Scoring guide:** 9–10 → move on. 6–8 → review wrong ones before Session 2. ≤5 → re-watch "Basic ratios" and redo.
